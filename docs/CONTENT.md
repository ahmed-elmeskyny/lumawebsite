# Luma Website Content

This document is the source of truth for customer-facing English copy on the
Luma website.

Use it together with `CLAUDE.md`, `BRAND.md`, and `PRODUCTS.md`. Shopify remains
the source of truth for live prices, variants, availability, cart, and
checkout. Policy language must follow the final approved business rules.

## Status and Usage

This document is a review draft until the user approves it.

After approval:

- Copy in quotation marks is approved for use.
- Small changes for grammar, responsive length, or accessibility are allowed
  only when they preserve the meaning and voice.
- Do not invent replacement slogans, product claims, promotions, or policies.
- Do not publish internal notes, bracketed placeholders, or unresolved items.
- If live Shopify data conflicts with a price, size, or availability statement,
  show the Shopify value and report the conflict.

## Copy Principles

- Write in clear, natural English.
- Keep headlines short and expressive.
- Use `you` and `your`.
- Be playful without becoming childish.
- Keep price, size, delivery, COD, cart, and checkout language literal.
- Use sentence case for headings and buttons unless an approved artwork asset
  uses another style.
- Prefer `socks`, `pair`, `box`, and `edition` over vague luxury language.
- Use `MAD` after the amount: `80 MAD`, not `MAD 80`.
- Write size ranges as `EU 36–40` and `EU 41–46`.
- Write `cash on delivery` in full on its first important appearance. `COD` may
  be used only as supporting shorthand.
- Do not use fake urgency, fake scarcity, unapproved discounts, unsupported
  superlatives, or unverified product-quality claims.

## Accessibility Copy Rules

- Link text must describe its destination; avoid repeated `Click here`.
- Buttons must describe their action.
- Error messages must explain what happened and how to continue.
- Form labels must remain programmatically available even when the visual
  design uses placeholders.
- Product alt text must identify the actual product, view, and relevant
  edition; do not stuff marketing language into alt text.
- Decorative mascot and pattern assets should use empty alt text.

Example product alt text:

- `Color Your Steps open box with Kickflip Luma, Luma Doodle, and Watch Your Step socks`
- `Healthy Shifts closed rigid magnetic box`
- `Hypno Wave sock, front view`

## Content Still Blocked

The following must be resolved before the relevant content or page is
published:

- Final delivery areas and estimated delivery times
- Return, exchange, cancellation, and refund rules
- Customer-support email, phone, WhatsApp, and operating hours
- Final materials, construction, fit, and care information
- Legal entity name, business address, and legal contact details
- Privacy policy and terms reviewed for the actual Shopify setup
- Newsletter provider, consent wording, and privacy link
- Verified Instagram profile URL
- Any launch date, promotion, discount, free-shipping rule, or scarcity message

Until these are approved, Claude must omit the affected claims, keep unfinished
policy routes out of public navigation, and report the blockers.
